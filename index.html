PHASE 1 · BASE
Wks 1–5 · Jun 7–Jul 12

Build consistency and easy aerobic fitness. Adapt joints and tendons. Most runs should feel very manageable — if you’re breathing hard, slow down.

PHASE 2 · BUILD
Wks 6–11 · Jul 13–Aug 23

Increase endurance. Long runs grow, Tuesday Run Club gets purposeful. You’ll start feeling more “runner fit” by the end of this block.

PHASE 3 · PEAK
Wks 12–17 · Aug 24–Oct 4

Your longest runs. Your hardest weeks. This is where sub-2:00 is won. Fuel properly, sleep well, and keep Monday & Friday genuinely easy.

PHASE 4 · TAPER
Wks 18–20 · Oct 5–17

Volume drops sharply. Your legs will feel weird — heavy, restless, flat. That’s the taper. Trust it. You are not losing fitness. You are storing energy.

Race Day Strategy

Start first 3km at 5:50/km — slower than goal pace. Resist the adrenaline surge.

Km 4–15: settle into 5:38–5:42/km. Don’t panic over individual splits.

Km 16–21.1: if you have anything left, use it now.

Fuel: water at every station. Gel around km 9–10.

Volleyball & Running

Tuesday + Thursday volleyball counts as real athletic load. Don’t underestimate it.

Monday and Friday must stay easy. That balance is what keeps you healthy through October.

Weekly mileage will go from ~15–20 km/week early to ~30–40 km/week at peak — right-sized given volleyball is also taxing your legs.

When Things Go Wrong

Missed a run: Skip it, don’t double up.

Feeling off: Slow down 30 sec/km.

Niggle or pain: 3 easy days. If it persists, see someone.

Taper madness: Heavy weird legs in weeks 18–20 = normal. Trust it.
